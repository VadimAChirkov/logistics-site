Road freight

Road transport is the most flexible way to deliver cargo "door to door", indispensable over short and medium distances. Below are materials on consignment documents and the regulatory framework for road carriage.

Consignment documents

CMR (international), the TN consignment note (domestic) and the electronic consignment note (e-CMR / ETrN).

Regulatory framework

CMR Convention, TIR Convention, Russian rules for road carriage of goods and the e-CMR regulatory framework.
