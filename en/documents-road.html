Road transport documents

For road carriage the following are used: the CMR — for international carriage, the TN (consignment note) — for domestic carriage, and the ETrN — the electronic consignment note (mandatory in Russia from 2026).

CMR — the international consignment note

The CMR is the consignment note used for international road carriage between the countries party to the CMR Convention (1956). It is completed for goods in international road carriage.

  • Confirms the conclusion of the contract of international road carriage.
  • Is not a document of title (unlike the B/L).
  • Is usually issued in 3–4 copies (for the consignor, consignee, carrier, for customs/settlement).
  • Contains: the consignor, consignee, carrier, goods, weight, number of packages, loading/unloading points, conditions.

TN — the consignment note (domestic carriage in Russia)

The TN (consignment note) is the principal document for the carriage of goods by road within the Russian Federation. Its form is approved by the Rules for the Carriage of Goods by Road (a Russian Government resolution).

  • Issued for each domestic road carriage.
  • Contains information on the consignor, consignee, carrier, goods and route.
  • Confirms the fact of concluding the contract of carriage and acceptance of the goods.
  • Serves as the basis for settlement and for writing off carriage expenses.

ETrN — the electronic consignment note

The ETrN is a consignment note generated in the form of an electronic document. From 1 September 2026 it is mandatory for all participants in commercial road freight carriage in Russia: the paper note is prohibited, except for the exceptions established by law.

Key concepts (art. 2 of the Charter)

  • Electronic transport documents (EPD) — the electronic consignment note (ETrN), the electronic waybill, the electronic work order, the electronic accompanying statement, the electronic chartering contract, the electronic order, the electronic request, electronic freight-forwarding documents.
  • GIS EPD — the state information system for electronic transport documents (a federal GIS). It receives EPD from operators, processes and stores them, and provides them to state authorities (the Federal Tax Service, the traffic police, Rostransnadzor, etc.).
  • IS EPD — an information system for electronic transport documents ensuring the exchange of EPD between participants.
  • IS EPD operator — a legal entity included in the register of operators of information systems for electronic transport documents.
  • Participants in information exchange — the consignor, the carrier, the consignee, the charterer, the charterer's counterparty and other persons who have concluded an electronic document exchange agreement with an operator.

How the electronic document flow works

The ETrN scheme looks like this:

  1. Participants (consignor, carrier, consignee) connect to any IS EPD operator and conclude with it an electronic document exchange agreement (a public contract).
  2. Each party generates its own title (section) of the note in its IS EPD and signs it with an electronic signature.
  3. The IS EPD operator ensures the exchange of documents between participants (including through roaming between different operators).
  4. The operator submits the generated ETrN and the information from it to the GIS EPD.
  5. From the GIS EPD, the data is available to state authorities (the Federal Tax Service — for VAT and expense control; the traffic police/Rostransnadzor — during roadside checks).

Participants may use different operators: the document passes through each one's system and is joined at the GIS EPD level. An IS EPD operator cannot refuse to exchange documents merely because the counterparty uses another operator.

Who the operator is

  • The GIS EPD operator — the Federal Tax Service (FNS of Russia): administers the state system and maintains the register of IS EPD operators.
  • IS EPD operators — commercial electronic document exchange (EDO) operators included in the FNS register. The largest: "Tensor" (SBIS), "Kontur" (Diadoc), "Takskom", "Kaluga Astral", "L-Trans"/"Logistika" and others.
  • Participants choose an operator freely; for the ETrN one can use both the operators' specialised transport solutions and integration through one's own accounting system (ERP/TMS).

Electronic signature and titles

  • Signature: an enhanced qualified electronic signature (UKEP) — for legal entities and individual entrepreneurs; for natural persons an enhanced non-qualified signature (UNEP) via "Gosuslugi" is possible.
  • MChD (machine-readable power of attorney) — for signing by employees on behalf of an organisation.
  • ETrN titles: the consignor completes and signs its title, the carrier its own (including notes on acceptance and delivery of the goods), the consignee its own (confirmation of receipt). The document is "assembled" from the titles as the carriage progresses.

Obligation

From 01.09.2026 the consignment note is generated as an ETrN (art. 8 of the Road Transport Charter as amended by 140-FZ). No transition period is provided; the period until 1 March 2027 is not a deferral. The paper note is permissible only in the cases established by Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 262 of 01.06.2026.

When the note is issued on paper (Order of the Ministry of Transport No. 262 of 01.06.2026)

The paper form is permissible if two conditions are met simultaneously (part 1 of art. 8 of the Road Transport Charter):

  1. the situation falls under one of the cases listed below;
  2. the contract of carriage does not provide for an ETrN, and special rules do not establish the obligation to draw one up.

Cases of issuing on paper:

  • military goods are carried by road, where the consignor or consignee is the Russian Ministry of Defence, the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Russian National Guard, the FSB, the Emergencies Ministry, the Foreign Intelligence Service or the Federal Guard Service;
  • the consignor or consignee is a foreign legal entity whose information is not included in the state register of accredited branches and representative offices (art. 21 of Federal Law No. 160-FZ of 09.07.1999);
  • cabotage carriage of goods is performed by foreign carriers registered in an EAEU member state;
  • there is no internet access — the fact of the lack of connection must be recorded by an act (date, time, place, persons present with their positions, how the lack of connection manifested itself). When the connection is restored — return to EPD: the IS EPD operator will transfer the accumulated EPD to the GIS EPD;
  • the consignor and the consignee are the same person;
  • the consignor or consignee is a natural person and the carriage is not related to their business activity;
  • precious metals and precious stones are carried;
  • there is no technical possibility to send electronic documents to the GIS EPD due to a failure recorded by the state system for detecting, preventing and eliminating the consequences of computer attacks;
  • carriage in a territory where high-alert, emergency, martial law or counter-terrorist operation regimes are in force;
  • special cargo is carried;
  • periodicals are carried;
  • military and dual-use products are carried within military-technical cooperation and export control.
Roadside checks: in the absence of internet or a failure, the driver presents the paper transport document; in an abnormal situation in the GIS EPD — the EPD through software integrated with the IS EPD, or a paper copy of the EPD with an electronic signature stamp.

Liability

  • For the absence of a consignment note (including an electronic one), when issuance is mandatory — a fine on the driver of RUB 500 (cl. 2 of art. 12.3 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences).
  • There are currently no separate fines for non-compliance specifically with the electronic form.
  • The fact of issuing the note on paper does not in itself entail a risk of the tax authority refusing to recognise profit tax expenses and VAT deductions, if the other conditions are met (Letter of the Federal Tax Service of Russia No. SD-36-3/9573@ of 03.11.2026).

The format of the electronic consignment note is approved by Order of the Federal Tax Service of Russia No. ED-7-26/1065@ of 09.12.2021. For the information support of the management of the transport and logistics complex, the "GosLog" platform has been created.
