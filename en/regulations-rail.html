Rail freight: agreements and rules

SMGS (since 1951)

The SMGS is the Agreement on International Goods Transport by Rail. It establishes uniform rules for the carriage of goods between the participating countries (CIS, the Baltics, China, Mongolia, North Korea, Vietnam, Iran and others). It defines the SMGS consignment note, the procedure for accepting/delivering goods, the liability of the railways and the claims procedure.

CIM / COTIF (CIM Convention)

CIM are the Uniform Rules concerning the Contract of International Carriage of Goods by Rail, part of the COTIF Convention. They are the equivalent of the SMGS for the countries of Western and Central Europe. For carriage between different legal systems, the combined CIM/SMGS consignment note is used.

OTIF

The Intergovernmental Organisation for International Carriage by Rail — the administrator of the COTIF Convention and the CIM rules. The equivalent of the role that the OSJD plays for the SMGS.

OSJD

The Organisation for Cooperation between Railways — it administers the SMGS and other agreements within the framework of the CIS/Asian railways.

Russian Railways Charter

The federal law governing rail carriage within Russia: the contract of carriage, the rights and obligations of the parties, the carrier's liability and the claims procedure. It is supplemented by the Rules for the Carriage of Goods by Rail.

Practical significance: for export from Russia to Europe, the route often runs under two legal systems: within Russia — the SMGS, and after the bogie change at the border — CIM/COTIF. The combined CIM/SMGS consignment note allows the whole carriage to be documented by a single document.
