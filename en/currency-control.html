Currency control

Currency control is the state's supervision over settlements between residents and non-residents under foreign-trade contracts. Its purpose is to prevent unjustified capital outflows and to ensure the repatriation of foreign-currency earnings.

Transaction passport → UCN

The transaction passport (TP) is a historical currency-control document that has been abolished. It was replaced by the registration of the contract with an authorised bank and the assignment of a unique contract number (UCN).

  • UCN — a number that the bank assigns to a contract upon registration (Instruction of the Bank of Russia No. 181-I).
  • Who completes it: the resident does not "fill in" any forms himself — he submits to the bank the details of the contract and a copy of it, and the bank registers the contract within one working day and issues the UCN.
  • A separate "passport" in the form of a paper form is no longer required — registration is kept in the bank's system.

Contract registration thresholds

The threshold is determined by the amount of obligations converted at the CBR exchange rate on the date the contract is signed (effective from 2026):

Type of contractMandatory registration threshold
Exportfrom RUB 10m
Importfrom RUB 3m
Loan agreement (borrowing)from RUB 3m
Below the threshold the contract is not registered and no UCN is required — but settlements still pass through the bank with the indication of operation-type codes.

How the currency-control system is structured

The system is two-tier:

  • Currency-control bodies — the Government of the Russian Federation and the Bank of Russia (CBR). They set the rules and exercise supervision.
  • Currency-control agents — directly check operations and documents:
    • authorised banks — the principal link: they register the contract, assign the UCN and control payments;
    • the FCS (customs authorities) — reconcile data with the goods declarations;
    • the FTS (tax authorities);
    • VEB.RF and professional securities-market participants.

Documents and reporting

  • SPD — a certificate of supporting documents: submitted to the bank for each delivery/payment within the established time limits.
  • Bank control statement (VBK) — generated by the bank for a registered contract.
  • Liability — art. 15.25 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences: a breach of currency legislation entails heavy fines (up to 100% of the amount of the unlawful operation).
Practical advice: watch the deadlines for submitting the SPD and the correspondence between the payment amount and the value of the goods in the declaration. A discrepancy is a frequent cause of fines in currency control.
