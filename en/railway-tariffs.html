Rail freight tariffs

The charge for carrying goods on the RZD (Russian Railways) network is state-regulated and rests on two principal documents — Tariff Guide No. 1 (tariffs for the carriage of goods and infrastructure services) and Tariff Guide No. 2 (charges for the use of wagons and containers). From 1 January 2026 the tariff framework was renewed: the former Price List No. 10-01 (approved by FEC of Russia Resolution No. 47-t/5 of 17.06.2003) ceased to apply and was replaced by a new tariff calculation procedure.

Tariff Guide No. 1 — the tariff calculation procedure

Tariff Guide No. 1 was approved by FAS of Russia Order No. 894/25 of 06.11.2025 "On approval of the procedure for calculating tariffs for the carriage of goods by public rail transport and services for the use of public rail transport infrastructure" (registered with the Ministry of Justice on 22.12.2025 No. 84708; as amended by FAS of Russia Order No. 306/26 of 24.04.2026). It entered into force on 01.01.2026 and replaced Price List No. 10-01.

Chapter I. General provisions

  • Scope: the tariffs apply on all sections of the RZD infrastructure of 1520 mm and 1435 mm gauge accepted into permanent operation, and are mandatory and uniform for all users of RZD services.
  • Types of traffic: tariffs for the carriage of goods apply in all types of traffic (except transit, unless otherwise provided by international agreements of the Russian Federation).
  • Time of calculation: the tariff is determined as of the date of the calendar stamp in the transport documents — at the station of departure (including for imports under a through international consignment note), the transshipment station or the station of re-issuance of the note.
  • Tariff distance: calculated under Tariff Guide No. 4 (Protocol of the 31st session of the Council for Railway Transport of the CIS member states of 15.02.2002) and the Procedure for determining the shortest distance (Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 313 of 12.09.2024) — bypassing low-traffic sections and high-speed lines.
  • Special cases: direct international/non-direct traffic via border crossings (calculation to/from the state border), carriage involving infrastructures not owned by RZD, and carriage over several unconnected sections of infrastructure.

Chapter II. Tariffs for the carriage of goods

The carriage charge is determined by the type of consignment tendered under the note: wagon, group, block-train (route), container, small, consolidated wagon or a consignment in a train formation not owned by the carrier.

  • Wagon consignment — goods for which a separate wagon must be provided.
  • Group consignment — goods tendered under one note, for which more than one wagon is required but fewer than a block-train consignment.
  • Block-train (route) consignment — goods tendered under one note, for which the number of wagons meets the norms of a sender's route (by weight or length).
  • Small consignment — goods for which a separate wagon is not required.
  • Consolidated wagon consignment — goods of different consignors/consignees carried in one wagon.

Tariff calculation algorithm: the type of consignment is determined → the ETSNG (Unified Tariff and Statistical Nomenclature of Goods) item and the weight of the goods → the tariff class of the goods → the tariff scheme number for the given type of consignment → the base rate of the tariff scheme multiplied by the correction coefficients.

Chapter III. Tariffs for infrastructure services

Tariffs for services in the use of the RZD infrastructure (empty-wagon runs, infrastructure services in carriage) are calculated separately.

Annexes: ETSNG and tariff classes

The ETSNG — Unified Tariff and Statistical Nomenclature of Goods — contains 12 sections (agricultural products; timber, woodworking and pulp-and-paper products; metal ores; fuel and energy products; mineral raw materials and construction materials; metallurgical products; machinery; chemical products; food products; light and printing industry products; other goods; organic chemistry products).

Under the ETSNG item, each good is assigned a tariff class — first, second or third (the first class has the lowest tariffs, the third the highest). Coefficients to tariffs for the carriage of individual goods (in loaded and empty runs) are set separately.

Group and block-train (route) consignments

Group and block-train consignments occupy a special place: on the one hand, they are types of consignment by which the tariff is calculated (Tariff Guide No. 1), on the other — a technology whose organisation is governed by separate Rules for the carriage of goods by sender's routes. Both parts are brought together below.

Basic definitions (Art. 2 of the Russian Railways Charter)

  • Group consignment — a consignment of goods whose carriage requires more than one wagon but less than a block-train consignment.
  • Block-train (route) consignment — a consignment of goods whose carriage under one consignment note requires wagons in a number meeting the norms established for sender's routes by weight or length.
  • Sender's route — a train of a weight or length established by the carrier, formed by the consignor on a non-public track or under a contract with the carrier on public tracks, with the obligatory condition of releasing in transit at least one technical station from the re-marshalling of such train (Art. 13 of the Charter).

Tariff part (Tariff Guide No. 1)

The type of consignment is established under clause 16.2 of TG No. 1. For wagon, group and block-train consignments, the tariff class of the goods (under the ETSNG) is determined.

Recording in the consignment note (field 38 "Type of consignment"):

  • "ВО" — wagon consignment;
  • "ГО" — group consignment (tendered under one consignment note);
  • "М" — block-train (route) consignment.

Organisational part — Rules for the carriage of goods by sender's routes

Until 01.03.2027, the Rules for the carriage of goods by sender's routes on rail transport (Order of the Ministry of Railways of Russia No. 10Ts of 29.03.1999, as amended — Orders of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 94 of 10.06.2009 and No. 258 of 03.10.2011) are in force.

From 01.03.2027, the new Rules for the carriage of goods and empty freight wagons by rail, establishing the basic conditions and procedure for organising the carriage of goods by sender's routes, come into force (Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 238 of 27.05.2026, registered with the Russian Ministry of Justice on 09.07.2026 No. 87429; in force until 01.03.2033).

Key provisions of the new Rules (No. 238):

  1. Basis — a contract between the carrier and the consignor (sender) and/or the owner of a non-public track (or the infrastructure owner), a train of established weight/length with the obligatory release of at least one technical station from re-marshalling under the train formation plan.
  2. An application to organise a route is submitted to the carrier (electronically with an e-signature or on paper); it is reviewed within up to 30 calendar days; the consignor is notified of the result by registered letter or e-mail.
  3. Types of routes (from one departure station): direct (to one destination station, addressed to one or several consignees) and with splitting at stations determined by the infrastructure owner.
  4. Core and attached part: where there is a point of change (a station where the weight/length is reduced), the route consists of a core (follows without re-formation to the destination station) and an attached part (to the point of change in the route, then in a freight train).
  5. Agreement on acceptance — the consignor agrees the acceptance procedure with the consignee and sends written consent to the carrier; before carriage, an application for carriage (Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 216 of 17.07.2025) or a request-notification for empty wagons (Order No. 374 of 07.12.2016) is submitted.
  6. Recording of documents — under one or several notes, in accordance with Rules No. 256 (of 27.07.2020) and the Rules for the carriage of goods by groups of wagons under one note (Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 32 of 26.02.2015).
  7. Carrier verification — when a route is tendered, the carrier may verify that the actual weight/length of the train meets the norms.

Tariff Guide No. 2 — charges for the use of wagons and containers

Tariff Guide No. 2 — Rules for the application of charges for the use of wagons and containers of the federal rail transport (approved by FEC of Russia Resolution No. 35/12 of 19.06.2002, as amended on 29.04.2015). It sets the charge for the time wagons and containers spend with the consignor/consignee (under loading, unloading, waiting, etc.).

  • Table No. 1 — base rates for the use of wagons.
  • Table No. 2 — base rates for the use of containers.
  • The rates are differentiated by groups of wagons/containers and intervals of payable time of use; the charge is determined by multiplying the base rate by coefficients.

Tariff indexation from 01.10.2026

By FAS of Russia Order No. 612/26 of 03.08.2026 (registered with the Ministry of Justice on 26.08.2026 No. 87988), from 01.10.2026 the following were indexed:

  • Tariff Guide No. 1 (tariffs and charges, chapters II–III) — factor 1.085 (not applied to the declared-value charge rate — item 46 of chapter II);
  • Tariff Guide No. 2 (base rates for the use of wagons and containers) — factor 8.437;
  • Tariff Guide No. 3 (charges for additional operations) — factor 9.435;
  • charge for the presence of rolling stock on public tracks — factor 2.663;
  • base rates for special rail carriage — factor 2.443.
In practice: to calculate the exact cost of carriage one must successively determine the type of consignment, the ETSNG code and tariff class of the goods, the tariff scheme, and apply the current indexation factors. The current rate tables are published on the official resources of RZD and the FAS of Russia.
