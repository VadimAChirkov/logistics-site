Non-tariff regulation in the EAEU

Non-tariff regulation (NTR) is a set of state measures regulating foreign trade that are not customs-tariff measures (i.e. they are not limited to the payment of duty). It is a permissive regime: to import or export certain goods, it is not enough to pay the duty — one must obtain a licence or permit, or confirm compliance with technical requirements. Within the EAEU, non-tariff measures are uniform for all member states.

A related but separate subject is export control: the national system of the Russian Federation for the control of dual-use goods and technologies (Federal Law No. 183-FZ). A separate section «Export control» is devoted to it.

Legal basis

  • Treaty on the EAEU (signed 29.05.2014, in force since 01.01.2015), Annex No. 7 "Protocol on Non-Tariff Regulation Measures in Respect of Third Countries";
  • Decision of the EEC Board No. 30 of 21.04.2015 "On Non-Tariff Regulation Measures" (as amended on 06.10.2026) — approves the unified list of goods subject to non-tariff measures in trade with third countries;
  • sectoral acts on technical regulation and sanitary, veterinary and phytosanitary measures.

Technical regulation: CU TR → EAEU TR

Technical regulations are the main non-tariff instrument for mass-market goods. They were originally adopted within the Customs Union and were called CU TR (TR CU); since 2016 new regulations are adopted as EAEU TR (TR EAEU); the earlier CU TRs remain in force alongside the EAEU TRs. A regulation establishes mandatory safety requirements for products and is adopted by the Eurasian Economic Commission (EEC).

The unified EAC mark

Products that have passed conformity assessment to the EAEU TR requirements are marked with the unified circulation mark EAC (Decision of the Customs Union Commission No. 711 of 15.07.2011). Without this mark, goods cannot be placed on the EAEU market.

Two forms of conformity assessment

FormWho confirmsWhen it applies
Certificate of conformityan accredited certification body (tests + production assessment)higher-risk products (children's goods, electrical equipment, PPE, etc.)
Declaration of conformitythe applicant themselves on the basis of evidence (own, or with a body's involvement)the remaining products subject to conformity assessment

  • details of issued certificates and registered declarations are held in the Unified Register (tech.eaeunion.org).

Licensing and the permissive procedure

The list of goods whose import/export requires a licence or permit is established by Decision of the EEC Board No. 30 of 21.04.2015 (sections "Licensing" and "Permissive procedure"). In the Russian Federation licences are issued by the Ministry of Industry and Trade of Russia, and permissive documents are issued by the relevant authorities (Roszdravnadzor, Roskomnadzor/GRFC, the FSB, etc.).

Practical importer/exporter checklist: before starting a transaction, check the goods in order: (1) whether they fall under the unified NTR list (prohibitions, licensing, quotas) — EEC Decision No. 30; (2) whether they are subject to mandatory conformity assessment and under which TR — whether a certificate or declaration is required, and the EAC mark; (3) whether an SGR / veterinary / phytosanitary certificate is required; (4) whether the goods are subject to the permissive procedure (radio-electronics, etc.). The absence of a required permissive document means the customs authorities will refuse to release the goods.
