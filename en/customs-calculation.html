Calculation of customs payments

Below is the algorithm for calculating customs duty and VAT on the importation of goods into the customs territory of Russia, with a real example.

Calculation algorithm

  1. Determine the customs value (CV) = price of the goods + cost of carriage to the EAEU border + insurance + other costs directly related to the goods before crossing the border (packaging, containers, commissions, licence fees, etc., where not included in the price).
  2. Calculate the import duty = CV × duty rate (by TN VED code).
  3. Calculate VAT = (CV + duty) × VAT rate (at the current rate).
  4. Total payments = duty + VAT (+ customs fees for operations).

The procedure for determining the customs value is set out in Chapter 5 of Federal Law No. 289-FZ of 03.08.2018 "On Customs Regulation in the Russian Federation" (taking into account Chapter 5 of the EAEU Customs Code).

An important nuance: the customs value includes carriage only up to the EAEU border. Carriage within the customs territory (for example, Vladivostok → Moscow) is not included in the CV where it is itemised separately in the documents and not included in the price of the goods. Including the full freight to the final destination in the CV is a frequent mistake leading to overpayment of VAT.

A real example

Product: Deye SUN-6K-SG05LP1-EU-SM2-P hybrid solar inverter, 6 kW.

Delivery: Shanghai → Moscow, one 20-foot container.

Initial data

ParameterValue
TN VED code8504 40 850 0 (inverter)
Import duty rate0%
VAT rate22%
Value of the goods in the containerRUB 3,453,750
Freight Shanghai → VladivostokUSD 3,500
CBR exchange rate (on the settlement date)82.9803 RUB/USD

Step 1. Freight in rubles

3,500 × 82.9803 = RUB 290,431.05

Step 2. Insurance premium

It is the goods that are insured, not the transport service: the cost of carriage is not included in insurance where the transport services are itemised separately in the documents. Therefore the insurance base = value of the goods = RUB 3,453,750.

Insured amount = base × 110% = 3,453,750 × 1.10 = RUB 3,799,125.

Insurance premium = insured amount × 0.5% = 3,799,125 × 0.005 = RUB 18,995.63.

Step 3. Customs value

CV = goods + freight to the border + insurance = 3,453,750 + 290,431.05 + 18,995.63 = RUB 3,763,176.68.

Step 4. Import duty

CV × 0% = RUB 0.

Step 5. VAT

(CV + duty) × 22% = 3,763,176.68 × 0.22 = RUB 827,898.87.
