Experimental Verification of TU, MTU, NTU

Before a new method of stowage and securing of cargo is approved, it undergoes an experimental verification confirming that the securing method proposed in the project withstands the real loads arising when a train is in motion. The procedure is established by section 12 of Chapter 1 of the Technical Conditions for the Stowage and Securing of Cargo in Wagons and Containers (approved on 27.05.2003 No. TsM-943, as amended on 23.10.2017).

Three stages of verification

The experimental verification comprises three successive stages:

  1. Impact tests — a mandatory stage;
  2. Running tests — mandatory where reusable and reusable (inventory) securing devices are used; in other cases the need for them is determined by the commission;
  3. Trial carriage — a mandatory stage.

Who initiates the verification and how

The verification is carried out on the basis of a decision of the authority approving the TU, MTU, NTU (in accordance with section 7 of Chapter 1). That authority establishes the composition of the commission, the timing and location of the verification (public or non-public tracks), the procedure for allocating and supplying the wagons and containers, the polygon (route) for running tests, and the procedure for accompanying the wagons.

The decision to carry out the experimental verification is taken no later than 30 days from the date of receipt of the TU, MTU, NTU project.

Between three and five wagons (or coupled wagons, or containers) loaded in accordance with the TU project are subjected to the verification.

The commission includes specialists from the departments of the approving authority, representatives of the departments monitoring train traffic safety, and representatives of the developer of the TU, MTU, NTU, the consignor and the owner of the infrastructure on whose territory the verification is carried out. The commission is headed by a representative of the authority responsible for freight and commercial operations.

The commission ensures that the condition of the cargo and its securing conforms to the project requirements, that the verification procedure is observed, that stage reports and a conclusion on the reliability of the method are drawn up, and that proposals for the adjustment of the TU, MTU, NTU are developed.

Stage 1. Impact tests

Preparation includes a trial loading in accordance with the project and the application of control marks to the cargo and wagon, fixing the initial position of the cargo.

The tested wagon is rolled into a "wall" — a group of no fewer than three gondola cars loaded to full capacity with inert cargo (for example, sand, crushed stone). On a straight section of track, 12 impacts are performed at increasing speeds:

For coupled wagons with the cargo resting on two wagons, an additional 10 impacts are performed on a curved section (radius 300–400 m) at speeds of 4–8 km/h.

The speed of the wagon before the impact is determined from the time taken to pass a 10-metre control section: v = 36 / t (t — time in seconds).

Passing criterion: the stowage and securing method is deemed to have passed the tests if, after 10 impacts (up to 8 km/h), the securing devices show no substantial defects and the cargo remains in a secured condition suitable for transport, and after impacts at 8–9 km/h no damage to the wagon is recorded.

If the displacement of the cargo or damage to the securing devices threatens traffic safety or the safety of the cargo and rolling stock, the tests are immediately terminated with a corresponding entry in the report.

Stage 2. Running tests

The purpose is to verify the reliability of the securing method under real conditions of train movement. The polygon must contain several curved sections of 350 m radius and must have no speed restrictions for freight trains.

The tests are carried out by a separate train in daylight; the commission is accommodated in a wagon directly behind the locomotive. Running tests include several journeys:

SpeedMinimum mileage
up to 90 km/hno less than 100 km
110 km/hno less than 60 km

At the commission's request, the speedometer tape of the locomotive is attached to the running-test report. If damage to the securing devices or displacement of the cargo threatening safety is detected, the tests are immediately terminated.

Stage 3. Trial carriage

Trial wagons are included in trains on a general basis. The total mileage during trial carriage must be no less than 1500 km. Carriage may be single or repeated (over a specified period).

In the upper right part of the front side of the consignment note, the endorsement "Trial carriage" is made, and a trial-carriage report is attached to the note.

Where carriage is accompanied, the members of the commission systematically inspect the cargo and its securing, recording the results in the trial-carriage log (the amount of displacement is measured from the initial position). Where carriage is unaccompanied, the departure station sends a telegram to the destination station regarding a commission unloading.

At the destination station, unloading is carried out under the supervision of the carrier and a representative of the consignee; the trial-carriage report is completed and signed within 3 days from the day of unloading and sent to the authority that decided to conduct the verification.

Result of the verification

On the basis of the reports, the commission, no later than 30 days from the day of receipt of the last trial-carriage report, takes a decision on the suitability of the stowage and securing method under verification, formulates remarks on the project and the deadlines for their implementation. The decision is formalised as a conclusion. On its basis, the consignor or developer adjusts the TU, MTU, NTU project and submits it for approval and confirmation.

Practical tip: experimental verification is a long and resource-intensive process (impact tests, running tests, trial carriage with a total mileage of 1500 km or more). If you plan to carry cargo by a method not provided for by the TU, allow a time reserve for the development and verification of the MTU/NTU — from submission of the project to the conclusion there are at least two 30-day control periods, not counting the tests themselves.
