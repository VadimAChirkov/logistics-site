VAT on export from and import to the Russian Federation

Taxation at the 0% rate applies to the following sales (cl. 1 of art. 164 of the Russian Tax Code):

1. Exported goods

The 0% rate applies to goods:

  • removed under the export customs procedure;
  • placed under the free customs zone customs procedure;
  • removed under the re-export procedure (including products of processing in the customs territory, waste and residues);
  • removed from a free customs zone / free warehouse;
  • removed from the territory of the Russian Federation to the territory of an EAEU member state (under the EAEU Treaty of 29.05.2014);
  • sold by Russian organisations or individual entrepreneurs to natural persons from abroad (from a warehouse in a foreign state), with delivery to an address outside the EAEU.

The condition is the submission to the tax authorities of the documents provided for by art. 165 of the Russian Tax Code.

2. Services for the international carriage of goods

International carriage is carriage by sea-going or river vessels, vessels of mixed (river–sea) navigation, aircraft, rail transport and road vehicles, in which the point of departure or the point of destination is located outside the territory of the Russian Federation.

The 0% rate extends to the services of Russian organisations and individual entrepreneurs:

  • for the provision of railway rolling stock and/or containers for international carriage;
  • freight-forwarding services (FFS) under a freight-forwarding contract for the organisation of international carriage.

What is included in freight-forwarding services

Negotiations on sale-and-purchase contracts, completion of documents, acceptance and delivery of goods, organisation and performance of carriage, delivery and removal of goods, loading/unloading and warehouse services, information services, cargo insurance, payment and financial services, customs clearance, forwarding escort, development and agreement of technical conditions for loading and securing of cargo, tracing of cargo, control of the completeness of shipment, re-marking, servicing and repair of containers, servicing of refrigerated containers, and storage of goods.

⚠️ When the 0% rate does NOT apply. The zero rate does not extend to services for the carriage of exported/imported goods, or to FFS for such carriage, if simultaneously:
1) the organisation/individual entrepreneur performs the carriage only between points in the Russian Federation;
2) the organisation/individual entrepreneur is not indicated in the transport, consignment and/or other documents confirming the export/import of goods as one of the carriers.

Particulars of rail carriage

  • The 0% rate does not apply to the services of Russian rail carriers indicated in subcl. 9 of cl. 1 of art. 164 of the Russian Tax Code.
  • It applies to services in the organisation and performance of rail carriage from the place of arrival of the goods in the Russian Federation (from ports or border stations) to the station of destination in the Russian Federation.

3. FFS and railway rolling stock on export (within Russia)

The 0% rate applies to FFS and services for the provision of railway rolling stock and containers for the carriage of exported (re-exported) goods by rail, provided that the point of departure and the point of destination are in the Russian Federation.

4. Inland waterway transport

The 0% rate applies to the works (services) of inland waterway organisations in respect of goods removed under the export/re-export procedure, for carriage within the Russian Federation from the point of departure to the point of unloading or transshipment onto sea-going vessels or other modes of transport.

5. Air carriage (transit with a landing in Russia)

The 0% rate applies to services for the carriage of goods by aircraft, where the point of departure and the point of destination are outside the Russian Federation, with a landing in the Russian Federation, if the place of arrival of the goods in the Russian Federation and the place of departure from the Russian Federation coincide.

Documents to confirm the 0% rate (art. 165 of the Russian Tax Code)

For services for the international carriage of goods (subcl. 2.1 of cl. 1 of art. 164 of the Russian Tax Code) the taxpayer confirms the right to the 0% rate by submitting to the tax authority (cl. 3.1 of art. 165 of the Russian Tax Code):

  1. a contract (copy of the contract) for the provision of the services — with a foreign or Russian person;
  2. copies of the transport, consignment and/or other documents confirming the export of the goods from the Russian Federation (import into the Russian Federation) — unless otherwise provided by subcl. 3 of cl. 3.1 of art. 165.

Carriage with EAEU countries

For export from the Russian Federation to the territory of an EAEU member state, or import from the EAEU territory (by sea, river, aircraft, rail and road transport), copies of transport documents are submitted indicating the place of discharge or loading (station of destination/departure) in the territory of the other EAEU state.

Rail carriage from the place of arrival to the station of destination in Russia

For the organisation and performance of rail carriage from the place of arrival of the goods in the Russian Federation (from ports or border stations) to the station of destination in the Russian Federation, the following are submitted:

  • a register in electronic form with information from the documents under which the goods arrived at the port / border station, plus information from the consignment document on the carriage to the station of destination;
  • a register with information on the registration number of the transit declaration or the goods declaration.
Time limits and nuances: the procedure with electronic registers for rail (subcl. 3 of cl. 3.1 of art. 165) was introduced by Federal Law No. 92-FZ of 22.04.2024 and applies to tax periods beginning from 01.04.2025. In the event of a discrepancy in (or absence of) information, the tax authority may request copies of documents — they are submitted within 30 calendar days from the date of the request (cl. 1.2 of art. 165 of the Russian Tax Code).

Case law on art. 165 of the Russian Tax Code

The highest courts consistently state: in deciding on the 0% rate, the economic substance of the transaction (its link to export) is decisive, not the formal conformity of the documents. A formal approach to refusing the 0% rate is impermissible.

Key acts of the highest courts

  • Ruling of the Constitutional Court of the Russian Federation No. 31-P of 30.06.2020 (the "Gazpromneft Trading GmbH" case): the 0% rate applies even if the taxpayer provided services only at the domestic stage of international carriage due to unforeseen circumstances beyond the parties' control (a vessel accident, transshipment, cancellation of the bill of lading) — provided the link to the completed export transaction is confirmed. A cancelled bill of lading does not lose its evidentiary value.
  • Resolution of the Plenum of the Supreme Arbitration Court of the Russian Federation No. 65 of 18.12.2007: documents with insignificant defects of form or content cannot be regarded as not submitted; the significance of the defects is assessed by the court. This is the basis for confirming the 0% rate through the court.
  • Resolution of the Presidium of the Supreme Arbitration Court of the Russian Federation No. 6031/08 of 11.11.2008: a taxpayer cannot be fined for non-payment of VAT if the export is confirmed after 180 days — the grounds for a fine under art. 122 of the Russian Tax Code cease to exist.
