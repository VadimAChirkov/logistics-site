Road freight: conventions and rules

CMR Convention (1956)

The Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road. It governs international road carriage between the contracting states. Key provisions:

  • Establishes the uniform CMR consignment note.
  • Defines the carrier's liability: presumption of fault and a limit of liability.
  • Limitation period — one year.
  • Russia is a party to the CMR Convention.

TIR Convention (1975)

The Customs Convention on the International Transport of Goods under Cover of TIR Carnets. It allows goods to be carried across borders without intermediate customs inspection in a sealed vehicle until the destination is reached.

Russian rules for the carriage of goods by road

A Russian Government resolution defining the procedure for domestic road carriage: the form of the consignment note (TN), the procedure for its completion, loading/unloading rules and the liability of the parties. On its basis, the mandatory electronic consignment note (ETrN) has been introduced from 2026.

From 01.09.2026 the consignment note is generated as an ETrN. The cases where it is issued on paper are defined by a separate order of the Russian Ministry of Transport.

Freight-forwarding documents (electronic)

From 01.09.2026 freight-forwarding documents are also generated in electronic form (art. 2 of Federal Law No. 87-FZ as amended by 140-FZ) and submitted to the GIS EPD. The cases where they are issued on paper are defined by Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 115 of 03.04.2026 (as amended 31.08.2026; registered with the Ministry of Justice on 26.05.2026 No. 86642; valid until 01.09.2032).

7 cases in which freight-forwarding documents are issued on paper:

  1. goods for the needs of state authorities, national defence, state security and law and order, special and military cargo;
  2. in territories under high-alert or emergency regimes, a state of emergency, martial law, or the legal regime of a counter-terrorist operation;
  3. the client has no technical possibility to transmit an electronic document due to force majeure;
  4. the freight forwarder has no technical possibility to transmit an electronic document due to force majeure;
  5. services to a natural person for personal, family or household needs (not business activity);
  6. the client is a foreign legal or natural person;
  7. carriage of military and dual-use products within military-technical cooperation and export control (item 7 introduced by Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 389 of 31.08.2026).

The "GosLog" platform and the register of freight forwarders

140-FZ also introduced new mechanisms for regulating freight-forwarding activities:

  • Register of freight forwarders — information on legal entities and individual entrepreneurs engaged in freight-forwarding activities is placed in a register of notifications. The right to operate arises from the day of inclusion in the register. Existing forwarders must file a notification within 60 days (from 01.03.2026).
  • The "GosLog" platform — the national digital transport and logistics platform of Russia (a federal GIS, a "single window" principle). The operator is the federal executive authority in the transport sphere (the Russian Ministry of Transport) or a subordinate organisation.
  • Data storage — a freight forwarder must store in the Russian Federation information on contracts, cargoes and routes for three years.
Practical significance: international road carriage requires a CMR (plus a TIR Carnet for transit), while domestic carriage requires a TN/ETrN. Do not confuse them: the CMR applies to international carriage, the TN only within Russia.
