Export support in the Russian Federation

State support for exporters is a system of financial and non-financial measures aimed at promoting Russian non-commodity non-energy goods on foreign markets. The measures are implemented within the national project "International Cooperation and Export". The key operator of most programmes is the Russian Export Center (REC, VEB.RF Group), which acts as the agent of the Government of the Russian Federation.

Key institution: the Russian Export Center (REC)

The REC is the state institution supporting non-commodity non-energy exports. It supports companies at every stage of entering international markets, from market analysis to financial support for the transaction. The REC Group includes:

  • EXIAR (Russian Agency for Export Credit and Investment Insurance) — insurance of export credits and investments;
  • EXIMBANK of Russia (ROSEXIMBANK) — lending, guarantee and financial support for exporters;
  • "Export School" — educational programmes for exporters.

Most services are provided online on the state digital platform "My Export". The REC is also responsible for implementing the "Made in Russia" promotion programme.

Transport-cost compensation (logistics subsidy)

One of the most in-demand measures is compensation of a part of the costs of transporting goods for export. The legal basis is Government Resolution No. 496 of 26.04.2017 "On the provision of subsidies… to compensate part of the costs of transporting products". The operator of the measure is the REC.

In 2026 the list of subsidised products was significantly expanded: in addition to machine building and the timber industry, the support now covers the chemical and pharmaceutical industry, cosmetics, light industry and a number of other sectors.

Restrictions: the support does not cover the costs of supplies to unfriendly states, nor supplies to India carried out through the ports of the Azov-Black Sea basin.

Plans for 2027

The Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade plans to significantly increase funding for export-support measures:

  • funding for transport-cost compensation — up to ~RUB 16.2 billion (several times higher than in 2026);
  • an increase in the maximum subsidy per recipient — from RUB 200 million to RUB 300 million and above;
  • an expansion of compensable costs: product certification abroad, the establishment of service centres abroad, and the resumption of the corporate competitiveness enhancement programme (KPПK);
  • funding for the regional export-support infrastructure — 72 constituent entities of the Russian Federation.

The final terms will be determined after the federal budget and the Ministry's regulatory documents are approved.

What is NOT covered by the support measures

  • Commodity and energy exports (oil, gas, unprocessed metals as raw materials);
  • precious metals (gold, silver, etc.) — regulated by separate mechanisms: Ministry of Industry and Trade licensing, the priority offer to Gokhran, assay supervision;
  • for the transport subsidy — supplies to unfriendly states.

How to obtain support (step by step)

  1. Check eligibility — the products must relate to non-commodity non-energy exports and be included in the list of subsidised products;
  2. Register on the "My Export" platform (or in the GISP for individual measures);
  3. Prepare the documents — confirmation of production of industrial products in the Russian Federation, the export contract, documents on the actually incurred transport costs;
  4. Submit the application within the established acceptance windows (the selection is competitive);
  5. Wait for the decision and receive compensation after inclusion in the register of recipients.
Practical exporter checklist: (1) determine whether your products qualify as non-commodity non-energy exports; (2) check the current subsidy parameters for the year — budget, compensation rate, limit and application deadlines (they change annually); (3) apply via the "My Export" platform within the established windows; (4) watch for geographic restrictions (unfriendly states, individual routes); (5) keep documents confirming the actually incurred transport costs. Precious metals and raw materials are not covered by this measure.
