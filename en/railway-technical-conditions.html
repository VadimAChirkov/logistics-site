Technical Conditions for the Stowage and Securing of Cargo in Wagons and Containers (TsM-943)

The Technical Conditions for the Stowage and Securing of Cargo in Wagons and Containers (hereinafter — the TU, reference TsM-943) are the core regulatory and technical document of rail transport that establishes the procedure and conditions for the stowage and securing of cargo carried within the territory of the Russian Federation. The document was approved on 27.05.2003 No. TsM-943 and is in force as amended on 23.10.2017 (current version).

Purpose and scope

The TU establish the methods for the stowage and securing of cargo in universal four-axle wagons (gondola cars, flat cars, covered wagons) and in containers for carriage over railway tracks of 1520 mm gauge at speeds of up to 100 km/h inclusive.

Cargo whose mass and dimensions exceed the limits established by the TU is carried in accordance with the Instruction for the carriage of oversized and heavy cargo (for details see «Oversized and heavy cargo»).

The TU, MTU and NTU system

The TU form a single system with two complementary levels of technical conditions:

  • TU — the Technical Conditions themselves (TsM-943);
  • MTU (local technical conditions) — for cargo whose stowage and securing methods are not provided for by the TU; developed by the consignor and approved by the carrier;
  • NTU (non-standard technical conditions) — for methods not developed under either the TU or the MTU; applied for one-off or irregular carriage, developed by the consignor and approved by the carrier.

TU (TsM-943)

A TU project includes a descriptive part, stowage and securing schemes and a design-and-explanatory note; where the project is favourably considered, an experimental check of the proposed securing method is carried out (in accordance with section 12 of Chapter 1 of the TU).

MTU — local technical conditions

MTU are developed by the consignor for cargo whose stowage and securing methods are not provided for by the TU, and are approved by the carrier. The MTU package contains a descriptive part with a title page, stowage and securing schemes (including schemes for the return of reusable securing devices in the empty state) and a design-and-explanatory note.

  • approved MTU are registered in the MTU register with a number assigned; a copy is sent to the carrier;
  • MTU may be used by all consignors served by the carrier that approved them;
  • the validity period of MTU is 7 years; in the absence of remarks the term is extended by the carrier for a further 7 years.

NTU — non-standard technical conditions

NTU are developed by the consignor for one-off or irregular carriage, where the stowage and securing method is not developed under either the TU or the MTU. The NTU project is submitted to the carrier in four copies for approval; the approval is formalised by an act.

  • NTU are registered in the NTU register with a reference assigned (for example, NTU-17-001-17.04.03);
  • NTU may be used only by the consignor for which they were developed;
  • the validity period of NTU is 5 years, with the possibility of extension for a further 5 years.

The procedure for the development, approval and amendment of the TU, MTU and NTU is set out in detail in section 7 of Chapter 1 of the TU (clauses 7.1–7.3).

Structure of the document

The document consists of twelve chapters:

Chapter 1 — general requirements (12 sections)

Chapter 1 contains the general requirements and methods applicable to all cargo:

  1. General provisions — scope, the TU/MTU/NTU system, the relationship with the Instruction for the carriage of oversized and heavy cargo;
  2. Loading gauges — the basic, preferential and zonal loading gauges, their outlines and regions of application;
  3. Preparation of wagons and containers for loading — cleaning, closing hatches and sides, forming coupled wagons;
  4. Securing devices for cargo in wagons — tie-downs, lashings, couplings, bindings, wooden stanchions, bars, thrust shoes, etc., requirements for materials and securing fittings;
  5. Preparation of cargo for carriage, loading and unloading requirements;
  6. Stowage of cargo in wagons — permissible shifts of the centre of gravity, stowage of cargo;
  7. The procedure for the development, approval and amendment of the TU, MTU, NTU (clauses 7.1–7.3);
  8. Monitoring of compliance with the technical conditions for the stowage and securing of cargo;
  9. Knowledge testing of the TU for employees responsible for the stowage and securing of cargo;
  10. The calculation method for the stowage and securing of cargo in wagons;
  11. Peculiarities of the stowage and securing of long cargo in wagons;
  12. The method for conducting the experimental check of technical-conditions projects.
Practical tip: the endorsement on the reverse side of the consignment note confirming compliance with the TU must contain the numbers of the relevant chapter, section and figure of the TU; where securing is performed under the MTU/NTU, it must contain the number and date of approval of the MTU/NTU respectively. This is a mandatory condition for accepting the cargo for carriage — verify the endorsement before signing the documents (for details see «How to organise a rail shipment»).
