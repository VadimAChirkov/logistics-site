TN VED and the HS code

The HS (Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System) is an international classification developed by the World Customs Organization (WCO). It is used in more than 200 countries as the basis for customs tariffs.

The EAEU TN VED (Commodity Nomenclature of Foreign Economic Activity of the Eurasian Economic Union) is the national (regional) extension of the HS used in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Armenia.

Structure of the 10-digit code

The TN VED code consists of 10 digits divided into meaningful levels:

DigitsLevelExample (8517 12 00 00)
1–2Chapter85 — electrical machinery and equipment
3–4Heading8517 — telephones, communication apparatus
5–6Subheading (HS level, worldwide)8517 12 — for cellular networks
7–8EAEU subheading8517 12 00
9–10EAEU sub-subheading8517 12 00 00

How the nomenclature is formed

  • The first 6 digits are uniform worldwide — that is the level of the Harmonized System (HS).
  • The 7th–10th digits are refined at the EAEU level and are used to set the rates of duties, VAT, excises and non-tariff regulation measures.
  • The nomenclature contains 21 sections and 97 chapters (chapters 01–97); chapter 77 is reserved, and chapters 98–99 are for national use.
  • The nomenclature is revised approximately every five years (HS 2022 is in force, HS 2027 is in preparation).

Why correct classification matters

The TN VED code directly determines:

  • the rate of import/export customs duty;
  • the amount of VAT and excises;
  • non-tariff regulation measures — licensing, certification, quotas;
  • prohibitions and restrictions on import/export.
⚠️ A classification error is costly: incorrect classification entails additional charges, fines under art. 16.2 of the Russian Code of Administrative Offences, delay of the cargo, and administrative or even criminal liability. Classification is the most responsible part of declaration, which is why it is performed by experienced declarants or customs representatives.

How to classify a product

  1. Determine the functional purpose of the product and the material of manufacture.
  2. Find the appropriate chapter, then the heading and subheading.
  3. Use the Explanatory Notes to the TN VED (the official EEC reference) for accurate classification.
  4. In case of doubt, apply for a binding classification decision from the customs authority.
