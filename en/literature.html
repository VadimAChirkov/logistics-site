References and sources

Main literature

  • Limonov E. L. "Foreign trade operations of sea transport and multimodal carriage". Third edition, revised and supplemented. — St. Petersburg: Modul LLC, 2006.

Regulatory documents and rules

International trade rules

  • Incoterms 2020 — International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), Publication No. 723E.
  • UCP 600 — Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits, ICC, Publication No. 600.
  • ISBP 745 — International Standard Banking Practice for the examination of documents under documentary credits, ICC, Publication No. 745.

Sea transport

  • Hague Rules (1924) — the International Convention for the unification of certain rules relating to bills of lading.
  • Hague-Visby Rules (1968) — the Protocol amending the Hague Rules.
  • Hamburg Rules (1978) — the UN Convention on the Carriage of Goods by Sea.
  • Rotterdam Rules (2008) — the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Carriage of Goods Wholly or Partly by Sea.
  • SOLAS-74 Convention — the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea.
  • IMDG Code — the International Maritime Dangerous Goods Code.
  • Merchant Shipping Code of the Russian Federation (KTM RF) — Federal Law No. 81-FZ of 30.04.1999.

Rail transport

  • SMGS — the Agreement on International Goods Transport by Rail (in force since 1951; the CIS, the Baltics, China, Mongolia, North Korea, Vietnam, Iran and others).
  • CIM / COTIF — the CIM Uniform Rules concerning the Contract of International Carriage of Goods by Rail (the COTIF Convention).
  • Russian Railways Charter — Federal Law No. 18-FZ of 10.01.2003.
  • Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 191 of 19.06.2019 — Uniform forms of transport documents for the carriage of goods by rail (the GU-27u/GU-27u VTs consignment note). Registered with the Ministry of Justice on 06.09.2019 No. 55834; entered into force 365 days after publication.
  • Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 256 of 27.07.2020 — Rules for the carriage of goods and empty freight wagons by rail (the procedure for re-consignment, drawing up reports, completing the consignment note, storage periods for goods). Registered with the Ministry of Justice on 16.10.2020 No. 60411.
  • Instruction DCh-1835 — on the carriage of oversized and heavy cargo on the railways of the CIS member states and the Republics of Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia. Approved by the protocol of the XXX session of the Council for Railway Transport on 19.10.2001; amendments — the XLIV session of 13.10.2006 (as amended, in force since 01.01.2007).
  • Rules for the Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Rail (PPOG) — approved by the Council for Railway Transport of the CIS, protocol No. 15 of 05.04.1996, as amended (rev. of 06.11.2024).
  • Safety Rules and Procedure for the Elimination of Emergency Situations with Dangerous Goods (TsM-407) — approved by the Russian Emergencies Ministry on 31.10.1996 No. 9/733/3-2 and the Russian Ministry of Railways on 25.11.1996 No. TsM-407.

Air transport

  • Warsaw Convention (1929) — for the unification of certain rules relating to international carriage by air.
  • Montreal Convention (1999) — the modern convention that replaced the Warsaw system for most states.
  • Chicago Convention (1944) — on international civil aviation (ICAO).
  • IATA rules — the standards of the International Air Transport Association (AWB forms, e-freight, cargo handling).

Road transport

  • CMR Convention (1956) — on the contract for the international carriage of goods by road.
  • TIR Convention (1975) — the Customs Convention on the International Transport of Goods under Cover of TIR Carnets.
  • Road Transport and Urban Ground Electric Transport Charter — Federal Law No. 259-FZ of 08.11.2007.
  • Federal Law No. 140-FZ of 07.06.2025 (as amended 04.08.2026) — the transition to electronic transport documents and freight-forwarding documents.
  • Rules for the carriage of goods by road — Russian Government Resolution No. 2200 of 21.12.2020.
  • Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 115 of 03.04.2026 (as amended 31.08.2026) — the cases of issuing freight-forwarding documents on paper (registered with the Ministry of Justice on 26.05.2026 No. 86642).
  • Order of the Russian Ministry of Transport No. 389 of 31.08.2026 — amendments to Order No. 115.

Standard charter-party forms

  • BIMCO (Baltic and International Maritime Council) — the developer of standard charter-party forms and clauses.
  • GENCON, NYPE, BALTIME, SHELLTIME, ASBATANKVOY and others — the standard forms of chartering contracts.

International organisations

  • IMO — the International Maritime Organization.
  • ICC — the International Chamber of Commerce.
