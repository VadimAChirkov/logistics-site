Carriage of oversized and heavy cargo

The Instruction on the Carriage of Oversized and Heavy Cargo on the Railways of the CIS Member States, the Republic of Latvia, the Republic of Lithuania and the Republic of Estonia (Instruction DCh-1835) was approved at the XXX session of the Council for Railway Transport on 19 October 2001, with amendments at the XLIV session on 13 October 2006 (as amended, in force since 1 January 2007). It replaced Instruction No. TsD/4172 of 14.12.1983.

The Instruction establishes the procedure and conditions for the carriage of oversized cargo and of all cargo on special wagons (transporters) on 1520 mm gauge railways, as well as the procedure for agreeing the carriage of oversized, heavy and long cargo in traffic with third countries.

Loading gauge and oversized cargo

Cargo on open rolling stock within the 1520 mm gauge network must not exceed the basic loading gauge; for flat wagons and 4-axle gondola wagons a preferential loading gauge applies. The minimum permissible distance from the top of rail (ToR) level to the lower parts of the cargo (clearance) is not less than 150 mm.

Cargo is oversized if, when placed on open rolling stock, it exceeds the outline of the basic loading gauge, or if its geometric projections in curves exceed those of the reference wagon (frame length 24 m, wheelbase 17 m).

Cargo exceeding the limiting outline of the oversize zones (below 480 mm and above 5300 mm from ToR) is called super-oversized. Super-oversize above 5300 mm is vertical. Super-oversized cargo and cargo of the 6th degree of lower/side oversize is carried with a control frame.

Oversize index (5 characters)

For indication in the transport documents, an oversize index of 5 characters is introduced:

  • 1st character — always the letter "N" (oversize);
  • 2nd character — the degree of lower oversize (1–6);
  • 3rd character — the degree of side oversize (1–6);
  • 4th character — the degree of upper oversize (1–3);
  • 5th character — vertical super-oversize (8).

The absence of oversize in a zone is "0". Super-oversize in any zone is "8". Example: the index N8480 means lower and upper super-oversize, 4th-degree side oversize, no vertical super-oversize.

Heavy cargo

Heavy cargo is cargo whose mass or length (including in combination) or loads on the wagon frame (floor) exceed those permissible for carriage on universal rolling stock (Chapter 1 of the Rules for the Stowage and Securing of Cargo, Annex 14 to the SMGS).

Procedure for agreeing carriage (3 stages)

  1. First stage — preliminary agreement of the possibility, method and conditions of carriage on the basis of the consignor's application with the supporting materials.
  2. Second stage — agreement by the railway administrations of the technical documentation (drawings and calculations of the stowage and securing of the cargo).
  3. Third stage — final agreement of the documentation for cargo of high degrees of oversize and super-oversized cargo, as well as all cargo on transporters (the Special Carriage Department of the Central Directorate for Traffic Management of JSC "Russian Railways").
Practical significance: for the carriage of oversized or heavy cargo, the key points are the oversize index (N0000–N8888) and the completed stages of agreement. Without agreement with the railways and, where necessary, with JSC "Russian Railways", such carriage is not permitted. Special passage conditions (shunting operations, placing in trains) are observed at stations and on sections.
