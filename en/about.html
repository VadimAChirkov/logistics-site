About

My name is Vadim Alexandrovich Chirkov — an international logistics specialist with over 30 years of experience.

I graduated from the Ural Electromechanical Institute of Railway Transport Engineers in 1994 with a degree in "Organisation and Management in Railway Transport". In 2006 I obtained additional education in "Management" at the management training courses for the national economy at Perm State University.

Experience

My operational experience in logistics is over 30 years: railway transport management (my core speciality), international supply, supply chains and customs clearance. I have direct hands-on practice with all modes of transport — both in Russia and in the countries of Africa and the Middle East.

International projects

I have participated in a number of international projects:

  • Libya — construction of a high-speed railway;
  • Nigeria — the ALSCON aluminium production;
  • Dubai/Iraq — LUKOIL Overseas;
  • DR Congo — a trading company supplying mines "turnkey";
  • Guinea — a unique project to restore the FRIGUIA SA alumina plant;
  • Sierra Leone — a mining enterprise.

Digital tools and analytics

In addition to operational work, I have implemented digital tools:

  • a logistics module based on 1C (not an out-of-the-box version);
  • a transport module based on Microsoft Dynamics ERP — a working tool for expediting;
  • direct participation in the preparation of the implementation of a SAP TMS.

For data analysis I work fluently with Power Query and Power BI: I built daily KPI monitoring, a report on transit time and the share of logistics in the cost of delivery, and automated the forecast of payments to contractors 12 weeks ahead.

AI in my work

I actively use AI in my work: I have created and maintain two of my own AI agents (on a Raspberry Pi and a laptop, with memory synchronisation), and work with several language models. With their help I have automated routine tasks — document parsers (PDF→Excel, upload to MSD365), automatic calculation of the spot logistics budget, mailbox processing and others.
