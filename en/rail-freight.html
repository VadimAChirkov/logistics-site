Rail freight

Rail transport is a reliable way to deliver bulk cargo over medium and long distances, especially within the former Soviet space and on the China–Europe corridor. Below are materials on consignment documents and the regulatory framework for rail carriage.

Consignment documents

SMGS (international carriage) and GU consignment notes (domestic and export/import carriage within Russia), processing via the ETRAN system.

Regulatory framework

SMGS Agreement, CIM/COTIF, OTIF, OSJD, Russian Railways Charter.

Commercial report and general-form report

Cases of drawing up, by whom and where they are made, the signing procedure and the content of the reports.

Oversized and heavy cargo

Instruction DCh-1835: loading gauge, zones and degrees of oversized cargo, the H index, approval procedure.
