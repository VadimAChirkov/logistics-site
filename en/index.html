📈 Market indices — change

SCFI — Shanghai Containerized Freight Index

A composite container freight rate index published weekly (on Fridays) by the Shanghai Shipping Exchange. It reflects average spot rates for container shipments out of Shanghai on major routes (Europe, Mediterranean, the Americas and others). The key benchmark for export rates from China.

Drewry WCI — World Container Index

A composite container rate index by Drewry, published weekly (on Thursdays). It is calculated as the average rate (in USD) for a 40-foot container (FEU) across 8 key trade lanes. Widely used in index-linked freight contracts.

BDI — Baltic Exchange Dry Index

An index of dry-bulk freight rates published daily by the Baltic Exchange (London). It reflects rates for carrying dry bulk cargoes (ore, coal, grain) on major routes and consists of the Capesize, Panamax and Supramax sub-indices. A key indicator of the dry-bulk market.
